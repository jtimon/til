** CANCELLED #207 Consider removing Vec.move_from

   *Cancelled*: the premise described an implementation that no longer
   exists. Commit ~9fd09e6eb~ changed ~Vec.move_from~ from an
   element-copying realloc/memcpy operation into an O(1) transfer of the
   source's complete ~data/count/cap~ header. Commit ~d055a71bc~ then made
   the consumed side explicit through ~own~ and documented the ownership
   contract in [[file:../../src/core/vec.til][src/core/vec.til]]. ~Vec~ also no longer carries the
   cloned runtime element-type string mentioned by the original issue.

   Ordinary aggregate assignment is not an equivalent replacement.
   Assignment currently deep-clones aggregate identifier and field RHSes,
   then deletes the old destination. Replacing ~move_from~ is memory-safe
   at the audited sites, but would allocate a new buffer and recursively
   clone every live ~Expr~ before deleting the source. The current 35
   calls deliberately transfer rebuilt compiler vectors without that work:

   - 14 in ~desugarer.til~, 9 in ~typer.til~, 4 in ~loader.til~,
     3 in ~garbager.til~, 2 each in ~constfolder.til~ and
     ~scavenger.til~, and 1 in ~parser.til~.

   ~Expr.take_children~ is not redundant either. Its 10 callers use it to
   transfer parsed trees without cloning, and 4 append to an already
   populated AST (the initer and typer pending queues plus two incremental
   REPL paths). Assignment at those four sites would delete the existing
   program rather than append the new children.

   The helpers' current comments state their no-clone transfer and append
   contracts. Retaining both APIs is therefore the smaller and faster
   design; removing them would either change behavior or regress compiler
   allocation costs.
