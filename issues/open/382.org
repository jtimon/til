** WIP #382 Add concise construction of Some borrowed refs

*** Progress

Til forbids NULL refs, so wrapping an existing ref in an Option is always a
safe conversion. The current spelling repeats the complete destination type:

#+begin_src til
Option(ref TypeScope).Some(scope)
#+end_src

This is correct but noisy, and the pattern is not specific to TypeScope. Add a
generic concise spelling, provisionally ~safe_ref(scope)~, equivalent to
~Option(ref T).Some(scope)~ for any ~ref T~.

The design should:

- infer ~T~ from the argument without repeating the payload type;
- preserve the exact borrowed ref and its lifetime, with no clone, move, or
  ownership change;
- reject values that are not refs rather than introducing a nullable fallback;
- work in returns, arguments, assignments, and aggregate initializers; and
- choose a name that describes the operation clearly. ~safe_ref~ emphasizes
  that the source ref cannot be NULL; ~some_ref~ may more directly describe
  construction of the Option.

After choosing the spelling, replace the verbose generic pattern throughout
the codebase and add focused Option(ref T) coverage.
