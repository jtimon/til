** OPEN #510 Import public members of a namespace with ~Colors.*~

Today ~import(path, Name)~ accepts a top-level declaration, but the parser
rejects ~Colors.*~ with ~expected 'identifier', found '*'~. Qualified and
selective imports can also leave unselected public names visible by their
bare names. The GUI palette currently declares ~BLACK~, ~TEAL~ and its
other colors at module scope.

Implement the agreed distinction in [[file:../../doc/tutorial/8_modules_and_imports.org][Modules and imports]]:

#+begin_src til
import("../src/modes/gui", Colors)
ClearBackground(Colors.BLACK)
#+end_src

#+begin_src til
import("../src/modes/gui", Colors.*)
ClearBackground(BLACK)
#+end_src

Only the wildcard introduces all public members of ~Colors~ as bare names
in the importing file. It must not expose private members or unrelated
module declarations. Report a collision with a file's own ~BLACK~ at the
import; the qualified form lets both names coexist. Cover both forms and
their visibility and collision cases in interpreted and compiled tests.
Move the GUI palette into ~Colors~ and migrate GUI examples that use it.
