** OPEN #508 Separate the portable GUI facade from direct raylib access

~mode gui~ currently imports the vendored raylib bindings and adds til's
own palette, window, audio and file-dialog helpers. A program that wants
raylib directly (see ~examples/raylib.til~) has to repeat imports and
target-specific link directives to avoid taking the whole GUI facade.

Provide ~mode raylib~ for programs written against the vendored raylib API.
Keep ~mode gui~ as the portable til API: it can use raylib on targets where
that makes sense, while another target can implement the facade without
providing raylib. Keep GUI-only helpers and palette names out of the raw
mode, and share raylib link settings wherever both modes use it.

Acceptance: a direct raylib example builds without spelling out archive
paths or platform libraries; GUI examples continue to work on raylib-backed
targets and the TempleOS GUI path does not acquire a raylib dependency.
