** WIP #404 Simplify negated Boolean chains in source

De Morgan's laws let a Boolean chain use one negation instead of negating
every operand:

#+begin_src til
a.not().and(b.not()) == a.or(b).not()
a.not().or(b.not()) == a.and(b).not()
#+end_src

Prefer the source spelling with one trailing ~not()~ for longer all-negated
chains and all-negated subgroups of mixed expressions. Preserve operand order
and the short-circuit evaluation of ~Bool.and~ and ~Bool.or~. Keep ~neq~ and
~!=~ comparisons as comparisons instead of replacing them with ~eq.not()~.

The source cleanup does not require an automatic compiler rewrite. Adding a
compiler pass for this small simplification has a cost of its own; revisit it
only if measurements show a worthwhile benefit.
