** FIXED #124 Remove remaining manual frees for typed raw-buffer locals

Status update (2026-07-18): the original premise is obsolete. ~Dynamic~ never
owns; it is for type erasure and raw borrows. Issue #302 established the
replacement model, and #319 rejects user-facing allocator results bound to
~Dynamic~ or ~ref~ locals.

Allocator-rooted concrete locals such as:

#+begin_src til
buf : U8 = malloc(n)
#+end_src

are ordinary owned locals. Raw-allocation provenance makes them pointer-form
heap blocks, and the garbager inserts their plain ~free~ at last use and exit
edges. Source-level ~own~ is redundant on locals, so this issue does not
propose an owned-Dynamic rule, a new raw-buffer type, or an ~own U8~ local
spelling.

The remaining cleanup is narrower:

- Remove handwritten ~free(own buf)~ calls where ~buf~ is a concrete
  allocator-rooted local and automatic cleanup or an existing ownership
  transfer already handles it.
- Keep explicit frees that are real behavior: custom/manual delete
  implementations, foreign ownership, custom allocators, and buffers that
  intentionally do not use plain ~free~.
- Keep compiler-internal ~src/self~ erased ABI slots until #286 gives their
  ~write_ptr~ / interpreter heap transfers typed ownership. Those slots are
  not evidence that ordinary ~Dynamic~ values may own memory.
- Update ~doc/concepts/qualifiers.org~ and ~doc/c2til.org~ so they describe concrete typed raw
  locals and remove the old manual-free contract.

Issue #302 remains the ownership-pass source of truth. This issue is the
focused explicit-call and documentation cleanup after that machinery lands.

*** Post-mortem (2026-08-19)

The census is the finding: there was almost nothing left to remove. Of
269 handwritten ~free(own ...)~ calls in the tree, 28 name an
allocator-rooted local, and exactly ONE was redundant.

  - REMOVED: ~cloned_key : U8 = malloc(...)~ in ~DynMap.set~
    (src/std/dyn_map.til) -- a scratch buffer copied into the map's
    storage and then freed by hand. The garbager already inserts that
    free by raw_alloc provenance; verified by removing it and running
    test/dyn_maps.til under ASAN (4/4, no leak, no double free).
  - KEPT as real behaviour: ~buf : U8 = malloc(...)~ in
    src/modes/gui.til, which crosses into raylib as
    ~Wave(data = to_ptr(buf))~ and must outlive ~LoadSoundFromWave~.
    That is the "foreign ownership" survivor this issue anticipated.
  - KEPT by this issue's own rule: the 9 in ~src/self~ (the FFI
    marshalling slots -- ~raw~, ~atypes~, ~pdynamics~, ~cif~, ~args~,
    ~arg_ptrs~, ~arg_raws~, ~boxed_flags~), erased-ABI storage deferred
    to #286.
  - KEPT: the test-corpus frees, where the free IS the behaviour under
    test (script_dynamic_free.til, script_dynamic_global_ptr.til,
    aliases.til, and the deliberately-rejected cases in
    typer_errors.til).

The other acceptance criteria were already met before this commit, which
is worth recording so nobody re-does the search:

  - No owned-~Dynamic~ allocation convention survives in ordinary code:
    zero matches across src/core, src/std, src/modes and examples. The
    only hit in the tree is ~bad_raw_alloc_dynamic~ in
    test/typer_errors.til, where it is a REJECTED case.
  - The docs already describe the current contract:
    doc/concepts/qualifiers.org says a typed raw local's free is inserted
    by the garbager and "No hand-written free is needed", and
    doc/c2til.org's low-level memory section shows the same. Neither
    still carries the old manual-free contract, so neither needed an
    edit.

426/426 including the ASAN suite.

Acceptance:

- Census remaining handwritten frees of allocator-rooted locals and classify
  every survivor as required behavior or remove it.
- Ordinary user code contains no owned ~Dynamic~ allocation convention.
- Typed raw locals need no handwritten cleanup on normal or early-exit paths.
- The full ASAN suite passes after the removals.
